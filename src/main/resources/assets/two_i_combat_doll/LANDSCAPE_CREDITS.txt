Mountain landscape materials (1.2.0)

Current Cangsheng daytime dream realm:
Original editable Blender geometry and original procedural materials.
Terraced cliffs, blossom trees, waterfalls, violet-roofed castles, arcades and
floating islands were authored for this project. No source image, photograph,
external texture or external mesh is read by this authoring generator.
Background generator: tools/blender/dream_realm_panorama_j.py (J2)
Runtime stage/atlas generator: tools/blender/dream_realm_stage_k.py (K)
Reviewed integration: tools/combine-dream-realm-j2-k.py
The J2 panorama and K stage use separate editable Blender source scenes; the
integration copies their completed outputs without claiming one scene produced
all three resources. Exact per-source script, .blend and output hashes are in
the source_bakes records of the manifest below.
Panorama: textures/effect/cangsheng_landscape.png
Runtime geometry: models/effect/cangsheng_dream_stage.json
Runtime atlas: textures/effect/cangsheng_dream_materials.png
Authoring evidence and exact hashes:
verification/axolotl-20261027/dream-realm-bake-final.json
The editable .blend scenes, review images, authoring scripts and rejected historical
bakes remain outside packaged game resources. Project license: see LICENSE.

The licensed photograph supplies only weathered rock microstructure in the
offline crimson landscape bake. Mountain geometry, composition, palette, shadows and
animation are authored for this project. The source JPEG is kept in verification
with official metadata, hashes and the license record; it is not included in the
mod JAR. The derived crimson sky atlas is retained for that separate scene.
No pixels, models, audio or video frames from the user's reference media are
included in these assets. See tools/create-crimson-landscape.py for its
deterministic recipe. Earlier Cangsheng photo-assisted experiments are rejected
historical evidence, not the source of the current daytime dream-realm assets.
