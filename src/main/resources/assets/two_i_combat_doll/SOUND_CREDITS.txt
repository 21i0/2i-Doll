Cangsheng Axolotl — original synthesized sound set

cangsheng_awaken.ogg, revision 2: a finite low body hit and stable low stone
resonance, opening into a quiet pearlescent tail.
cangsheng_roar.ogg, revision 2: a low guardian vocal pulse with stable chest
resonance and a finite throat excitation; a fantasy creature sound, not a real
animal recording.
cangsheng_impact.ogg: short descending low hit, defined mid-frequency punch,
finite stone/crystal resonators and quiet reflection taps.
cangsheng_flute.ogg: a newly composed gentle three-note air-column phrase.

All four are original deterministic mathematical synthesis under the project's
MIT license. The current awaken and roar are generated by
tools/synth/original-cangsheng-audio-v2.py, with recipes, fixed seeds, listening
WAVs and exact encoded-asset hashes retained in
verification/axolotl-20261027/audio-v2/audio-audit-v2.json.
The unchanged impact and flute are generated by
tools/synth/original-cangsheng-audio.py; their original source evidence remains
in verification/axolotl-20261027/audio/audio-audit.json. Earlier four-cue OGGs,
generator and audit remain in that directory's original-v1-history/ folder.
No external recording, sample, reference-video audio or paid sound asset was
used. The dedicated packaged credit is
assets/two_i_combat_doll/CANGSHENG_SOUND_CREDITS.txt.

The new set is separate from the existing Kenney CC0 time_stop adaptation;
its original source credits and license remain as listed above.
